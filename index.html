How to use this site

How do I listen to a procedure?

Press play on the player. Only one procedure plays at a time. Audio loads when you press play, so the first seconds may take a moment on a slow connection.

What does the checkbox do?

It marks a procedure as listened on this device only. It is not sent to anyone and clears if you clear your browser data.

I can't listen right now. What can I do?

Open "Read transcript" under a procedure, where one is provided, or ask your supervisor for the written copy.
